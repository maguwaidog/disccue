# Commerce and disclosure operations

Owner-operated procedure, reviewed 2026-10-05. Do not store legal names, addresses, private telephone numbers, customer messages, license keys or payment details in this repository. This document describes the workflow, not individual customer records.

## Before enabling the website purchase journey

- Keep the software provider's correct legal name, actual business address and reachable telephone number ready outside this repository. The owner has adopted this preparation as the operating premise.
- Keep mmd.apps303@gmail.com monitored and able to receive requests from people who have not purchased. Keep the disclosed address and telephone number usable and current.
- Use the purchase information page, Terms and Privacy together. Do not describe the DiscCue brand as a registered corporate entity or substitute Polar's contact details for the provider's identity.

## Business information requests

1. Receive requests through mmd.apps303@gmail.com, including the suggested subject “DiscCue：事業者情報の開示請求” or “DiscCue: business information request.” A matching subject is helpful, not a condition of disclosure.
2. Do not require an order number, license key, purchase, payment or identity document. Disclosure is free and available before purchase.
3. Monitor the inbox regularly. Respond without undue delay and sufficiently before the requester decides whether to purchase. An automatic acknowledgement alone does not complete disclosure. Do not impose a purchase deadline while the person is waiting.
4. Reply by email with the correct legal name, actual business address and reachable contact telephone number. Include the current purchase information link so the recipient can review the product, price, payment, delivery and refund guidance.
5. Keep the request time, actual disclosure response time and completion status in access-controlled records outside the repository. Use the information only for the request and appropriate support records, as described in Privacy.
6. Keep contact information current. If the operation cannot provide the information promptly, stop directing new website visitors to purchase until the operation is restored; do not rely on the promise alone.

## Refund and transaction support

- Guide one-time purchase requests to support@polar.sh with the transaction date, order number and reason. Do not ask for license keys, full card data or unnecessary identity documents.
- Explain the 10-day period from the transaction date as a discretionary assessment route, not unconditional eligibility or a restriction of statutory rights. Do not automatically refuse an issue merely because ten days have passed.
- Record receipt and escalation times outside this repository. Cooperate with Polar, including supplying reasonably requested transactional support within 72 hours under its Master Services Terms section 6.2.
- Use only available relevant information and the customer's account of the issue; do not claim the app collects usage telemetry that it does not collect.
- Any agreed refund is processed through Polar. Do not issue a separate transactional invoice or refund by direct transfer outside Polar.
- Do not introduce an independent blanket no-refunds rule, a guaranteed refund window, or a trial-based waiver of consumer rights.

## Price and delivery maintenance

- Japan-facing checkout was observed before payment on 2026-10-05: JPY 2,480 total, tax included, one-time charge, no separate surcharge row. This is not a statement that a tax rate is permanently zero.
- The website states automatic license-key issuance after completed purchase, retrieval in the Customer Portal via order-confirmation email instructions, and the same installer for Trial and Standard. Do not promise a number of seconds or direct key inclusion in every email or confirmation page.
- If price, fulfillment, checkout or provider terms change, review all public wording before changing the purchase journey. Do not silently alter Polar settings as part of website maintenance.
