01 / THE PROBLEM

Your disc.
Your preferred subtitles.

An imported Blu-ray or 4K UHD may not include the subtitle language you need. DiscCue was made for viewers who want to keep using the original disc and their usual player, rather than move movie playback to a PC.

In the primary setup, your movie projector plays the film as usual. A second projector, connected to a Windows PC running DiscCue, places your own SRT subtitles on the same projection screen. You adjust the subtitle timing manually.

You provide the media and subtitles.

DiscCue does not provide movies, TV programs, Blu-ray/UHD content or subtitle files. It does not rip discs, decrypt them, host or stream copyrighted content. Use legally obtained media and subtitle files you are authorized to use.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Two projectors. One screen.

  1. Play the movie as usual.

    Your Blu-ray or UHD player sends the movie to your normal movie projector.

  2. Open your SRT in DiscCue.

    Load your SRT subtitle file on the Windows PC.

  3. Choose the subtitle projector.

    Select the second projector connected to your PC and open DiscCue's subtitle-only output.

  4. Place the subtitles on the same screen.

    Aim the second projector at the movie screen, then adjust subtitle position and timing manually.

Movie and subtitle playback are independent. If you pause or seek the movie, adjust subtitle playback yourself.

03 / FEATURES

Subtitle controls at your fingertips.

01 / YOUR SRT

Your subtitles, kept local

Load an SRT file you are authorized to use. Normal subtitle processing stays on your PC; there is no subtitle upload or cloud search service.

02 / SEPARATE OUTPUT

Subtitle-only output for a second projector.

Send subtitles to a second projector and adjust their font, size, weight, position and display width. A separate subtitle monitor is also available as an alternative setup.

03 / TIMING

Stay in control of timing

Use play, pause, offset and Seek / Jump to align the subtitles manually with the dialogue. Your existing player handles the movie.

04 / STANDARD

Current Line Sync

Align the current displayed cue to the subtitle playback clock. This Standard feature adjusts the overall offset without rewriting your SRT. It does not detect movie audio or synchronize automatically.

05 / OFFLINE

Standard works offline

Activate online once, then use Standard offline during normal subtitle playback. There is no periodic online revalidation. Deactivation needs an internet connection.

07 / FAQ

Questions before you start.

Do I need to read subtitles on a separate monitor?

Not in the primary setup. One projector shows the movie, while a second projector connected to your Windows PC places only the subtitles on the same projection screen. A separate subtitle monitor is an alternative, not a requirement for that two-projector setup.

Can DiscCue overlay subtitles directly on the movie playing on my TV?

Not currently. DiscCue does not intercept or modify the movie's HDMI signal, or insert subtitles into it. Its primary same-screen setup uses two projectors, one for the movie and one for subtitles.

What does DiscCue do?

DiscCue is a Windows app that loads your own SRT subtitles and sends them to a subtitle projector. In the primary setup, two projectors put the movie and subtitles on the same physical projection screen. You control subtitle styling, position, playback and timing manually. DiscCue does not play the movie or intercept or modify its HDMI signal.

Can I use external SRT subtitles with a Blu-ray player?

Yes, using a Windows PC and a second projector for subtitles. Your Blu-ray or UHD player continues playing the disc through the movie projector. Both projectors use the same screen. DiscCue does not send subtitles into the player or add a digital overlay to its HDMI signal.

Does it work with 4K or UHD Blu-ray movies?

The movie continues through your existing UHD playback equipment. DiscCue handles only the separate subtitle output, so it does not process the movie resolution, HDR or audio. It is not a disc player or ripping tool.

Do I need to play the movie on the PC or rip the disc?

No. You can keep playing the legally obtained disc on your existing equipment. DiscCue does not rip discs, decrypt them or stream copyrighted content.

Are movies or subtitle files included?

No. Supply legally obtained media and SRT files you are authorized to use. Subtitle downloads, translation and cloud search are not included.

How is the FREE Trial measured?

It provides 30 cumulative minutes of active subtitle projection. Loading, setup and paused or stopped playback do not consume it. Remaining time survives app restarts; projection playback pauses when the trial is exhausted. Current Line Sync is a Standard feature.

Is Standard a subscription?

No. Standard is a one-time purchase for up to two activated devices. Review the final price, currency and applicable taxes in Polar Checkout before paying.

How do I get my license key?

After your purchase is completed, Polar automatically issues the key. Follow the instructions in the order confirmation email to access the Customer Portal and view or copy it. Activate it inside the same DiscCue app; there is no separate Standard installer.

Can I move Standard to another PC?

Yes. Connect the current PC to the internet and deactivate it to release an activation slot, then activate the new PC. Up to two devices may be activated.

Can I use Standard offline?

Yes, after initial online activation. Normal subtitle use is offline, without periodic online revalidation. Initial activation and deactivation require an internet connection.

Is synchronization automatic?

No. Use playback controls, offset and Seek / Jump to align subtitles manually. Current Line Sync corrects the offset using the current displayed cue; it does not listen to the movie or control your player. Movie pause and seek are not followed automatically; adjust subtitle playback yourself.

Which Windows versions are tested?

Windows 11 x64. A Java runtime is included, so you do not need a separate JDK. Use the free trial to check your PC, display and SRT before purchasing.
