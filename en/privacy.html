Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

How DiscCue handles local subtitles, licensing, purchases and enquiries.

Subtitle files

SRT files are loaded and processed locally on your PC for display and timing adjustment. Normal subtitle use does not upload your SRT or movie. Users provide their own legally obtained media and authorized subtitles.

Activation and deactivation

Initial Standard activation sends your entered license key and activation information, such as the device label, to Polar over HTTPS. Licensing requests also handle license and activation identifiers. After activation, normal Standard use works offline without periodic online revalidation. Deactivation requires online communication.

See Polar's Privacy Policy for its handling of licensing information.

Checkout and payment

Purchases take place in external Polar Hosted Checkout. Polar Software, Inc., as merchant of record, handles email addresses, billing information and payment information. The DiscCue app, this website and the software provider do not directly process or store payment-card details.

The software provider may access some purchase and activation information made available by Polar for order or license management and support. Polar's storage and processing practices are described in its Privacy Policy.

Local storage

The app stores settings, remaining FREE Trial time, device-identification information and activated entitlement locally on your PC. This preserves remaining trial time across restarts and enables normal offline Standard use after activation.

This website

This is a static product website. Its code includes no analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, user accounts or payment processing. It does not set cookies or write to browser storage. The demo is a link to YouTube rather than an embedded player, and is not loaded automatically.

External services

GitHub Pages hosts the site. See GitHub's Privacy Statement for hosting-related data handling. When you follow the demo link, information handling on YouTube is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Checkout and licensing are subject to Polar's policies.

Support and business information requests

Email enquiries provide the sender's email address, message and any information they choose to attach. We use them to investigate and respond, support purchases and licenses, and maintain response records. Pre-purchase business information requests are handled as ordinary enquiries. No order number, license key or identity document is required for disclosure requests.

Contact us about access, correction, deletion or retention of enquiry information. For information held by Polar or another external service, also use that service's policy and contact channel.
