License delivery and activation

After your purchase is completed, Polar automatically issues your license key. Follow the instructions in Polar's order confirmation email to open the Customer Portal, then view and copy your key. No manual dispatch is required. Email delivery or system processing may take time. Contact DiscCue support if you cannot access the key.

Download the official DiscCue v1.0.1 installer and activate the purchased key inside the app. The FREE Trial and Standard use the same application; there is no separate Standard installer. Receipts and invoices record the purchase and are separate from the license key.

One license supports up to two activated devices. Initial activation and deactivation require internet access. Normal Standard use works offline after activation, without periodic online revalidation.

Before buying, check your PC and display setup using the FREE Trial: 30 cumulative minutes of active subtitle projection. Windows 11 x64 is tested, and a Java runtime is included; no separate JDK installation is required. The current Windows installer is not digitally signed, so Windows may display a security warning during installation. Trying or not trying the app does not restrict statutory rights or refund procedures.
