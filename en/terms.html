Intended use

DiscCue is a Windows subtitle display and projection application. Load your own authorized SRT and send it to a second projector aimed at the same physical screen as the movie projector. A separate subtitle monitor is a secondary setup. Subtitle playback and timing are manual and independent of the movie, including pause and seek. DiscCue is not a movie player or ripping tool and does not modify your video signal or control your playback equipment.

Movies, TV programs, Blu-ray/UHD content and third-party subtitle files are not included. DiscCue does not provide, host, stream or distribute them. You are responsible for obtaining and using your media and subtitles lawfully and respecting copyright and other rights.

Requirements and installer

The primary setup requires Windows 11 x64 (tested), Blu-ray / UHD playback equipment, a movie projector, a subtitle projector connected as a Windows PC display output, the same physical projection screen and your own SRT. A separate subtitle display is a secondary setup. Projectors and the screen are not included. A Java runtime is included; no separate JDK installation is required. The current Windows installer is not digitally signed, so Windows may display a security warning during installation.
